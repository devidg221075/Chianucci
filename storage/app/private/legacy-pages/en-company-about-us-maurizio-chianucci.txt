[h1] Chianucci MaurizioSince 1973 it has been operating in the production and sale of chains and bracelets.
[p] Founded in 1973, the CHIANUCCI MAURIZIO company operates in the production and sale of chains, bracelets, rings and earrings both in gold and silver, for the Italian and foreign market. With the collaboration of over 15 internal employees, as well as various craftsmen of semi-finished products. Over the years the company has gained its experience by developing various production lines including that of the hollow chain. Many of the main processing steps are completely handmade by qualified experts that cannot be replaced by machinery, thus further increasing the value of the products.
